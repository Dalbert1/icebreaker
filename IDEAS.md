## Trivia Categories

- **Region-tailored categories** — detect (or let the user set) their city/region and surface locally relevant trivia. A Tulsa deck could include Gathering Place history, Oklahoma music venues, local food spots, etc. Good for depth-of-connection and reducing generic feel.

- **User-created trivia** — let users write their own questions that people who match with them can select as a potential icebreaker category. A "Questions about me" category that functions as both trivia and a profile deep-dive. This doubles as a self-expression feature and a compatibility signal.
